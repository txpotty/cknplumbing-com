    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in Ketchikan

    Residential storm drain cleaning in Ketchikan typically runs $200 to $500 depending on the number of inlets and pipe length. Dry well cleaning and commercial systems typically cost more and are quoted after a site visit.

    We guarantee that the lines we clean will flow when we leave, and we stand behind our workmanship in writing. Crushed or broken pipe is identified and quoted separately.

    If our video inspection reveals that your storm drain is damaged, such as collapsed, cracked, or severely invaded by roots, cleaning alone will not solve the problem.     Our technicians are trained to service all types and brands of storm drain systems commonly found in residential and commercial properties. This includes standard PVC, corrugated HDPE, concrete, and cast iron drainpipes, as well as various types of catch basins and grates from manufacturers like NDS, ADS, and Zurn.

    We use water jetters, vacuum equipment and push cameras built for drain work. These tools remove debris without crushing thin-wall storm pipe.

    While you can remove surface debris from drain grates, deeply clogged storm drains need professional tools like hydro jetters and camera equipment for effective cleaning. DIY attempts can sometimes push debris further down or damage the pipes, leading to more costly repairs. It is safer and more effective to hire a professional.

    It is usually recommended to have your storm drains inspected and cleaned at least once a year. Properties with many trees or those that see frequent heavy rains may need twice-yearly cleaning to prevent buildup and blockages.

    Common signs of a clogged storm drain include standing water in your yard or driveway after rain, water overflowing from catch basins, unusual pooling near downspouts, or slow drainage in outdoor areas.
