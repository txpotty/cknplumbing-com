    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Ketchikan, AK

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Ketchikan.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Ketchikan, AK

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also look like slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is important if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Ketchikan home.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Ketchikan, AK

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Ketchikan, AK

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Ketchikan

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in many pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Ketchikan homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Ketchikan

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs fast attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep partial water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Ketchikan

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Ketchikan

    Slab leak detection in Ketchikan typically costs $150 to $450. Repairs range from about $800 for a simple reroute to $3,000 or more for direct access under finished floors. We provide a written estimate after locating the leak.

    Reroutes and repipes often require a plumbing permit, while small direct repairs may not. Requirements vary by city and county. We take care of permits and inspections when needed.

    For major slab leak repairs, especially those involving excavation or extensive re-piping, a plumbing permit from the local building department is often required. CKN Plumbing handles all necessary permitting processes so your repair is compliant with local codes and regulations, protecting your home.

    Once the slab leak is precisely detected, our technician will provide you with a detailed report and discuss the best repair options. These options may include trenchless repair, re-routing the affected pipe or traditional slab excavation and repair. We will then provide a transparent estimate for the repair work.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    Common signs of a slab leak include surprisingly high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring and a sudden drop in water pressure. Musty odors can also point to moisture buildup.

    CKN Plumbing relies on industry-leading brands for our slab leak detection equipment, including but not limited to LeakTronics, FLIR thermal cameras and specialized acoustic sensors.     Direct repair works well for a single leak on otherwise healthy pipe in an accessible spot. Rerouting is often better under tile or hardwood and avoids future leaks on that line. We help you weigh both.
