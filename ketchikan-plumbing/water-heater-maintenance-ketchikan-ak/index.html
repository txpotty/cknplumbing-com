    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Ketchikan, AK

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as necessary. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Ketchikan

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Ketchikan, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.
