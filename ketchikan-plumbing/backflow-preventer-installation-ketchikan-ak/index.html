    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Ketchikan

    Residential pressure vacuum breakers usually cost $250 to $700 installed. Double check and RPZ assemblies for larger homes or businesses range from $800 to $3,500 or more depending on size and enclosure needs.

    Constant dripping or discharge from an RPZ relief valve, low pressure downstream and a failed annual test are typical signs. Visible corrosion or a cracked body from freezing also means service is needed.

    We install all common types of backflow preventers, including reduced pressure zone assemblies, pressure vacuum breakers and double check valve assemblies. Our team will recommend the right device based on your property's hazard level and local plumbing codes in Ketchikan.

    Most water providers require testable assemblies to be tested at installation and then annually. Some high hazard facilities may be tested more often.

    Most municipalities require annual testing of backflow preventers by a licensed, certified backflow tester. This verifies the device is working correctly and continues to protect your potable water supply from contamination. We can also assist with scheduling these annual tests.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for new backflow assemblies, especially on commercial properties. We check local rules and pull the permit when needed.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, often just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.
