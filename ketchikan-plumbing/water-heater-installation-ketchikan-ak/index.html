    Expansion Tank Installation in Ketchikan, AK

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Ketchikan, AK

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, reliable and quiet.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Ketchikan

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Permits are commonly required for new fixtures, water heaters, boilers, and repiping, although inspection practices differ between urban and remote areas. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Ketchikan. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Ketchikan

Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a good choice for families with high hot water demand.
