    Compression Faucet Repair in Ketchikan

    Compression faucets, often found in older Ketchikan homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's usually due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks effectively. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Ketchikan, AK

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Ketchikan

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Ketchikan bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to stop leaks and restore full performance.
