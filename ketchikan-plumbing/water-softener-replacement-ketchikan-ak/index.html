    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Ketchikan

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the appropriate softening capacity in grains per day for your Ketchikan home, so the system performs well.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear out sooner with very hard or chlorinated water.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow.
