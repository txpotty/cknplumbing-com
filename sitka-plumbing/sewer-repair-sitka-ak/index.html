    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Sitka, AK

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Permafrost in colder parts of Alaska complicates any buried plumbing, and thawing ground can cause settling that cracks pipes. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Sitka, AK

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Sitka neighborhoods. Roots can work into tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe condition. For ongoing issues, we may recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Sitka

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.
