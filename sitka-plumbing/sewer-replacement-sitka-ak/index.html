    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Sitka

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup snags debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Sitka

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Sitka homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We thoughtfully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Sitka

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.
