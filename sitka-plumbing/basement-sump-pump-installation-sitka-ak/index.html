    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Sitka

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. If you leave a home unheated, draining the plumbing or keeping the heat on with someone checking in is strongly recommended. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.
