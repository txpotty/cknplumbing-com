    Underground and Under-Slab Plumbing in Sitka

    The underground phase sets the drain, waste and vent layout that every fixture in the house will depend on. We trench, bed and slope PVC or ABS drain lines at a minimum quarter inch per foot and protect every pipe that passes through the slab or foundation with sleeves. Frost heave is a major concern in Alaska, as freezing ground can lift and shift foundations, buried lines, and septic components. We compact bedding carefully and avoid rigid points that could stress the pipe as the ground moves. Each line is water tested and left visible for the inspector before your concrete contractor pours.
