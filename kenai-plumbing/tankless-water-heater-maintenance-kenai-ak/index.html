    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Kenai

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet.     Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Kenai

    The cost for tankless water heater maintenance in Kenai, AK typically ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's age, condition and the extent of descaling required.     Many manufacturers exclude scale damage from heat exchanger warranties. Regular, documented maintenance is the best way to keep your coverage intact.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds about an hour.

    CKN Plumbing services all major brands of tankless water heaters, including Rinnai, Noritz, Navien, Bosch, State, Rheem and more. Our technicians are trained on the specific maintenance requirements and diagnostics for a wide range of models to provide expert care for your unit.

    It is best to have your tankless water heater professionally serviced at least once a year. In areas with very hard water, or for heavy usage, more frequent maintenance, such as every six to nine months, might be worth it to prevent scale buildup and maintain efficiency.

    Many tankless water heater manufacturers require proof of regular, professional maintenance to keep your unit's warranty valid. Skipping annual service can cancel the warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs that would otherwise be covered. Always check your own warranty terms.

    Common signs include reduced hot water flow, fluctuating water temperatures, odd noises like banging or popping, error codes displayed on the unit, or a noticeable increase in your energy bills.
