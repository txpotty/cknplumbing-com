    Pool and Spa Heater Gas Lines in Kenai

    Pool heaters are among the largest gas appliances in a home, often rated at 250,000 to 400,000 BTU. They usually sit at the equipment pad far from the meter, so line size and length are crucial. We may recommend a larger main line or a meter upgrade from your gas utility. A properly sized line lets the heater reach its rated output and heat the water quickly.

    Gas Range and Cooktop Line Installation in Kenai

    Switching from an electric range to gas is one of the most frequent reasons homeowners call us. We run a dedicated branch from the nearest adequate supply, install a shutoff valve behind the range and connect with a new approved flexible connector. Professional-style ranges can need 60,000 BTU or more, so we confirm the branch and main can handle the load. The appliance is then checked for proper burner flame and lighting.

    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Kenai, AK

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most important steps in a successful tankless conversion.
