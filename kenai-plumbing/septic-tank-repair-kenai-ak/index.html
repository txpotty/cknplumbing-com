    Many households need pumping every three to five years, depending on tank size and household use. Regular pumping protects the leach field from solids.

    Watch for slow drains, gurgling pipes, sewage odors, high water alarms, wet spots over the field or sewage backing up into tubs. Any of these calls for a prompt inspection.

    To extend your septic system's life, keep up regular pumping every 3 to 5 years, avoid flushing non-biodegradable items, limit harsh chemical use, conserve water and keep vehicles off the drain field.
