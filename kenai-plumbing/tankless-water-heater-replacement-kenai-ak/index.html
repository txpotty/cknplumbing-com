    Most manufacturers offer 10 to 15 years on the heat exchanger and about 5 years on parts for residential use. We add our own labor warranty and register the unit for you.

    Repeated error codes, fluctuating temperature, water leaking from inside the cabinet and a unit older than 15 years are common signs. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is usually the better value.

    To protect the life and efficiency of your new tankless water heater, regular maintenance is key, especially in areas with hard water.
