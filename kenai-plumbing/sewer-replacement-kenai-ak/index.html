    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Kenai

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Kenai homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We thoughtfully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Kenai

    Many older homes in Kenai still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines, which are prone to root intrusion, corrosion, and cracking. CKN Plumbing can upgrade your system to modern PVC (polyvinyl chloride) piping. PVC is durable, resistant to root penetration, and has a smooth interior that lowers the likelihood of blockages, providing a long-lasting solution for your home's drainage.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Kenai, AK

    A cleanout provides simple access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on later maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Kenai, AK

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the extent of damage. Replacement ends recurring root problems.
