    French Drain Installation in Kenai

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after heavy rain. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or approved discharge point.

    Dry Well Installation in Kenai, AK

    Where there is no place to discharge water on the surface, a dry well lets it soak into deeper soil. Dense clay or hardpan layers can block absorption, so we test the soil and dig through hard layers when needed. Some jurisdictions have rules on dry well registration and placement, and we follow them. A properly sized dry well with a sediment chamber can handle back-to-back heavy storms.

    A blocked main sewer line can cause widespread backups throughout your home, usually due to tree root intrusion or sediment buildup. Our team uses heavy-duty equipment, including hydro-jetting, to blast through tough obstructions and thoroughly clean your main sewer line.
