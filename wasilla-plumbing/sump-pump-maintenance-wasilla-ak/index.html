    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Wasilla, AK

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Wasilla

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Wasilla due to their quiet operation and compact design.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Wasilla

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear.
