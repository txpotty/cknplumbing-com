    Trenchless replacements often take one day. Open-trench jobs usually take two to four days.

    We install PVC for open-trench work and HDPE for pipe bursting. Both are long-lasting and resist roots.

    Frequent sewage backups, multiple clogged drains, foul odors coming from drains or your yard, lush green patches in your lawn, indentations in your yard, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use are all strong indicators of a failing sewer line. Persistent problems often signal the need for replacement rather than just repairs.

    After replacement, minimize planting large trees or shrubs near the sewer line's path. Be mindful of what goes down your drains, and avoid flushing grease, wipes, and harsh chemicals. Regular professional inspections, especially if you have older pipes, can also help catch potential issues early.
