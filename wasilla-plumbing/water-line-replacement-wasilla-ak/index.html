    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Wasilla

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent of the damage. If your main water line in Wasilla has multiple leaks, severe corrosion, or is nearing the end of its lifespan, replacement is often the more cost-effective long-term solution. Our technicians will perform a thorough inspection, including pressure testing and possibly video inspection, to recommend the best course of action. We consider factors like the age of your home, the material of the existing pipe, and the nature of the damage before advising.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Wasilla

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and cost-effective choice for water line replacement in Wasilla. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for easier installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with corrosive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Wasilla

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Wasilla, AK

    Many homes have high street pressure that can stress pipes and fixtures. We install pressure reducing valves set to a healthy range. We also replace old gate valves with full-port ball valves at the house. This makes it easier to shut off water in an emergency. These upgrades safeguard your plumbing system.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Wasilla

    Copper has long been a popular material for water lines due to its durability, corrosion resistance, and longevity. CKN Plumbing specializes in installing new copper water lines, known for their ability to withstand high water pressure and limit bacterial growth. While a higher upfront investment, copper provides a dependable and long-lasting water supply system, a great choice for many homes in Wasilla, AK.
