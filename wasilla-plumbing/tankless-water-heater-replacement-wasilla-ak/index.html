    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Wasilla, AK

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units usually need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting.     Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Wasilla, AK

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Wasilla

    A like-for-like gas tankless replacement usually runs $2,800 to $4,500 installed, depending on the brand and venting. Converting from a tank to tankless often costs $4,000 to $6,500 because of gas line and venting upgrades. We give you a written quote before any work starts.

    Tankless water heater replacement is a complex job that involves gas lines, electrical wiring, water connections and venting. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional like CKN Plumbing due to the safety risks and potential for improper installation, which can lead to leaks, gas hazards or inefficient operation. Professionals also protect code compliance and warranty validity.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a new tankless water heater typically lasts 20 to 25 years. This is much longer than traditional tank water heaters, which usually last 10 to 15 years. Regular flushing and descaling can help extend the life of your tankless unit, especially in areas with hard water.

    Frequent error codes, fluctuating temperature, water leaking from inside the cabinet and a unit older than 15 years are common signs. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is usually the better value.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, usually takes 4 to 8 hours. If you are converting from a traditional tank water heater, the process can take longer, possibly a full day, due to extra plumbing, gas or electrical changes required for proper installation and venting.

    A direct swap usually takes four to six hours. A tank to tankless conversion with new gas and venting usually takes a full day.

    Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include frequent error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water. If your unit is over 15 to 20 years old and repairs are becoming common and costly, replacement is often the more sensible choice.

    DIY tankless water heater replacement is generally not recommended due to the complexity of gas lines, high-voltage electricity, water connections and venting requirements.
