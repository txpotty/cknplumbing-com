    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Wasilla

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Wasilla dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with persistent drain issues.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Wasilla, AK

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Wasilla neighborhoods. Roots can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe condition. For ongoing issues, we might recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Wasilla

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Wasilla, AK

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Wasilla.
