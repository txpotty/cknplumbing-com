    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back.     Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Wasilla

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system.     Gas Control Valve Replacement in Wasilla, AK

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Wasilla

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas.
