    Frequently Asked Questions About Pipe Replacement in Meadow Lakes

    The cost of pipe replacement in Meadow Lakes, AK varies widely depending on the extent of the damage, the type of pipes being replaced, and the materials chosen.     We mainly use high-quality PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for replacements.
