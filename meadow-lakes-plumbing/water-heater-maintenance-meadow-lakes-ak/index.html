    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Meadow Lakes

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Meadow Lakes

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than you might think. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is key for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Meadow Lakes

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.
