    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in College

    Locating a hidden slab leak takes specialized tools.     Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in College, AK

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to reduce wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in College, AK

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but requires skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in College, AK.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in College

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in College with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in College, AK

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most practical repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Alaska plumbing codes and the slab is properly restored, preventing future issues. We take great care to contain dust and debris during this process in your College home.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in College, AK

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Prompt action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in College

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in College with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Slab Leak Reroute in College

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.
