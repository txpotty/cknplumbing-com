    Tree roots are drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing serious blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.

    Caustic and acid cleaners can damage older cast iron, soften plastic joints and harm the person using them. They also seldom clear the full clog. Mechanical cleaning is safer and more effective.

    Water that drains slowly, gurgling sounds, sewer odors and water backing up into a tub or shower are the most common signs. If more than one fixture is affected, the problem is likely in the main line.

    In Badger, common causes include hair and soap scum in bathroom drains, grease and food particles in kitchen drains, and tree root intrusion into main sewer lines where mature trees grow close to the pipe.     Hydro jetting is best for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.
