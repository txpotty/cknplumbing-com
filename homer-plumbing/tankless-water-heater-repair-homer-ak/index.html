    Gas tankless water heaters require precise gas pressure and proper ventilation for safe operation. Issues can range from gas valve malfunctions to carbon monoxide sensor errors.     Pilot Light & Ignition Repair in Homer, AK

    For gas-fired tankless water heaters, a faulty ignition system can completely stop hot water production. Our team in Homer, AK diagnoses issues with igniters, flame sensors and gas valves. We confirm proper gas pressure and ventilation are maintained for safe and efficient operation.     Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Homer

    Tankless heaters rely on flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Homer

    When your tankless unit fails to produce hot water, it is often due to issues with the ignition, gas supply or a clogged heat exchanger. Our technicians in Homer will carefully diagnose the root cause, whether it is a faulty igniter, a pressure sensor fault or mineral buildup. We carry common replacement parts for fast fixes, restoring your continuous hot water supply efficiently. We also check for proper venting, which is essential for the safe operation of your unit.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Homer, AK

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a common hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights reliably.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Homer

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on hard water.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Homer, AK

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.
