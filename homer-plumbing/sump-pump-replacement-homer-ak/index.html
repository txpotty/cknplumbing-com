    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Homer

    A standard sump pump replacement usually costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps usually cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Homer homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    Sump pumps usually have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are important, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by CKN Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    We install a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you pick the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    While it may seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe and effective installation.
