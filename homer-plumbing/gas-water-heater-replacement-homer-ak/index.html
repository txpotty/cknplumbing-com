    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Homer

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can stress your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a properly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Homer, AK

    For homes in Homer, AK where traditional venting is not possible, or for those seeking higher efficiency, power vent gas water heaters are an excellent choice. These units use a powered fan to exhaust combustion gases, offering more flexibility in installation location. Our technicians are experienced in installing these more complex systems. That includes running new PVC vent piping and making proper electrical connections for the blower motor, all while following local codes.

    Atmospheric Vent Tank Upgrades in Homer, AK

    Most older homes have atmospheric vent water heaters that rely on natural draft up a metal flue. We inspect the flue pipe for rust, proper slope and correct connector size during replacement. A loose or undersized vent can spill exhaust into the garage or closet. We replace damaged vent sections and draft hoods as needed. Then we test draft with the burner running before leaving.
