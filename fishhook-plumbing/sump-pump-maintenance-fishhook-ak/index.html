    While you can perform basic checks like making sure the pump is plugged in and the discharge pipe isn't blocked, professional maintenance goes much deeper.     A standard sump pump maintenance visit typically takes about 30 minutes to an hour. If we find issues requiring repair, the time might be extended, but we will always communicate this upfront.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, hums without moving water, cycles very often or makes grinding noises. Rust, a stuck float or a musty smell around the pit are also warning signs.
