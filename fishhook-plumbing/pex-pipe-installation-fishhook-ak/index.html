    Why Choose CKN Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Fishhook, AK?

    PEX piping offers better freeze resistance than copper or CPVC. Homes here commonly use heat tape, insulated chases, and supply lines buried deep below the frost line to keep water flowing all winter. We make sure your new PEX system is installed correctly to get the most from that benefit.

    • Durability, PEX is highly resistant to corrosion, scale buildup and chlorine, common issues that degrade older metal pipes.
    • Scale Resistant, PEX does not collect mineral scale the way copper and galvanized pipe do.
    • Freeze Resistant, PEX can expand and contract, making it less likely to burst in freezing temperatures than rigid pipes.
