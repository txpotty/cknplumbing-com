    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Fishhook

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Fishhook

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Fishhook homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Fishhook

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward.     Sectional Point Repair in Fishhook

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.
