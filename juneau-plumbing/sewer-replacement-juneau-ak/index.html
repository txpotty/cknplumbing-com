    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Juneau, AK

    Many older homes in Juneau, AK still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time.     Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Juneau, AK

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, at the same time breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are well suited for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Juneau.

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the full extent of damage. Replacement ends recurring root problems.
