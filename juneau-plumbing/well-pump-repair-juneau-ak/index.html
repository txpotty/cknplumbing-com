    Pressure Switch Replacement in Juneau

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Juneau, AK

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. CKN Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Juneau operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.
