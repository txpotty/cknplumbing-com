    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Juneau

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines.     Root Intrusion Removal in Juneau, AK

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Juneau neighborhoods.     Sewer Camera Inspection in Juneau

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Juneau, AK

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Juneau.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Juneau

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    We work with all common types of sewer pipe found in Juneau and Alaska, including PVC, ABS, cast iron, clay and Orangeburg. Our technicians are experienced in repairing and replacing lines made from various materials, providing compatibility and durability with current plumbing standards. We also use high-quality, code-compliant replacement materials.

    For sewer line replacements, we primarily use durable, code-compliant materials such as PVC for its longevity and resistance to corrosion, or HDPE for trenchless applications. We select the best material based on your needs and local soil conditions.

    A small rental auger can clear a soft clog, but it will not fix a crack, belly or root intrusion. Forcing a cable through a broken pipe can make the damage worse. A camera inspection tells you whether you have a clog or a structural problem.

    Most spot repairs are completed in one day, including excavation and backfill. Larger replacements or jobs that need a city right-of-way permit can take two to four days. We give you a timeline before we start.
