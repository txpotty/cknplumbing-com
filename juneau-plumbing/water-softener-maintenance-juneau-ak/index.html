    Most visits take about an hour, including testing and a manual regeneration. Resin replacement can add another hour or a bit more.

    Resin often lasts 10 to 15 years, but chlorinated city water and iron can shorten that. Fine resin beads in your faucet aerators can signal resin breakdown.

    A standard water softener maintenance service usually takes about 1 to 2 hours. This can vary depending on the system's condition, age and whether any unexpected issues are discovered during the inspection.
