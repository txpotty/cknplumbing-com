    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Juneau, AK

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Juneau, AK, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device.     Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Juneau

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification.     An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic. A DCVA, or double check valve assembly, is used for low-hazard applications where contaminants are non-toxic. The choice depends on the risk of contamination at the cross-connection point.
