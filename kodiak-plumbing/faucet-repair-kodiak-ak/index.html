    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Repair in Kodiak

    Most faucet repairs cost $125 to $300, including the cartridge or seals and labor. Complex kitchen or touchless faucet repairs may run $200 to $400. We quote a flat price before any work starts.

    Low water pressure can stem from several issues, including mineral deposits clogging the aerator or internal components, a worn-out cartridge, issues with your home's main water supply, or even problems with municipal water pressure. Our technicians perform a careful diagnosis to accurately pinpoint and resolve the cause.

    Low pressure at one faucet is usually a clogged aerator or cartridge. Low pressure everywhere could mean a failing pressure regulator, a supply issue or old corroded pipes.

    Common signs include a persistent drip or leak, difficulty turning the handle, low water pressure, strange noises when the faucet is on, or visible corrosion around the base or spout. If you notice any of these issues, it's best to address them quickly to prevent further damage or water waste.

    We repair Moen, Delta, Kohler, Pfister, American Standard, Grohe, Hansgrohe and most other brands. We carry common cartridges on the truck.

    For a standard faucet repair, where only internal components are replaced and the fixture itself remains, no plumbing permits are typically required in Kodiak. However, if the repair involves altering the existing plumbing lines or installing a new type of fixture that requires new connections, a permit might be necessary. We will advise you if any permits are needed for your specific situation.

    If the faucet body is solid and parts are available, a repair is generally worth it. If it is corroded, cracked or discontinued, a new faucet is often the better choice.

    Simple cartridge swaps can be a DIY job if you identify the correct part. Stuck cartridges, corroded valve bodies and leaks under the sink are better handled by a plumber.
