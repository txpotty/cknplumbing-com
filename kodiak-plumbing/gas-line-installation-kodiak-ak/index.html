    Flexible Gas Line (CSST) Installation in Kodiak, AK

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing offers a flexible, practical option for gas line installation. CKN Plumbing is experienced with installing CSST, which can simplify routing and reduce the number of fittings compared to traditional black iron pipe. We make sure CSST is properly bonded and grounded, as Alaska code requires, to protect against lightning and electrical surges in your Kodiak home.

    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Kodiak, AK

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most critical steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    Fire Pit and Fireplace Gas Line Installation in Kodiak, AK

    Gas fire pits and fireplaces add comfort on cool evenings without the smoke and ash of wood. We run a properly sized line, install a key valve or shutoff and connect the burner assembly. Fire features need proper clearance from structures and plants, and we confirm placement before installation. Propane systems are set up with the correct regulator and orifice.

    Gas Range and Cooktop Line Installation in Kodiak

    Switching from an electric range to gas is one of the most common reasons homeowners call us. We run a dedicated branch from the nearest adequate supply, install a shutoff valve behind the range and connect with a new approved flexible connector. Professional-style ranges can need 60,000 BTU or more, so we confirm the branch and main can handle the load. The appliance is then checked for proper burner flame and ignition.

    Pool and Spa Heater Gas Lines in Kodiak

We may recommend a larger main line or a meter upgrade from your gas utility. A properly sized line lets the heater reach its rated output and heat the water efficiently.

    Outdoor Kitchen and BBQ Gas Lines in Kodiak
