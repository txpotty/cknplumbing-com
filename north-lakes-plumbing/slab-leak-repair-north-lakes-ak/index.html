    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in North Lakes

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic.     Emergency Slab Leak Repair in North Lakes, AK

    Slab Leak Reroute in North Lakes

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in North Lakes, AK

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in North Lakes, AK
