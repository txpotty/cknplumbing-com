    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in North Lakes, AK

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to verify flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and settling.

    Storm Drain Repair vs. Cleaning Costs in North Lakes, AK

    Understanding whether you need storm drain cleaning or a more extensive repair in North Lakes, AK can save you money. Cleaning typically costs between $200 and $600, addressing simple blockages. If cleaning does not resolve the issue, or if video inspection reveals cracks, collapsed pipes, or significant root intrusion, repairs might be necessary, costing $1,000 to $5,000 or more depending on the scope. We provide clear diagnostics to help you make informed decisions.

    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in North Lakes, AK

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for repair or replacement.

    Emergency Storm Drain Clearing in North Lakes

    Catch Basin Cleaning Services in North Lakes

    Catch basins are key parts of many storm drainage systems in North Lakes, designed to trap debris before it enters the main pipes. Over time, these basins can fill with leaves, trash, and sediment, reducing their effectiveness. Our service includes fully cleaning out catch basins, removing built-up material so they work correctly and prevent blockages further down the storm drain line, especially important in areas with mature trees.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in North Lakes

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during a downpour.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in North Lakes

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in North Lakes, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can infiltrate drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. CKN Plumbing uses dedicated root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can recommend further steps to prevent future root problems.
