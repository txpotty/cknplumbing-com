    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in North Lakes, AK

    Power outages can happen, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We offer and install battery backup sump pump systems that automatically activate if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in North Lakes, AK. Earthquakes are a real factor here, so flexible connectors and properly strapped water heaters help limit damage when the ground shakes. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in North Lakes

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by locating the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in North Lakes

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. Homes here commonly use heat tape, insulated chases, and supply lines buried deep below the frost line to keep water flowing all winter. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.
