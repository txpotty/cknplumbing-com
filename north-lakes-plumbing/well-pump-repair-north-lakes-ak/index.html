    Jet Pump Repair in North Lakes, AK

    Jet pumps, typically installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in North Lakes is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating efficiently again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Jet Pump System Repair in North Lakes, AK

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. CKN Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in North Lakes operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Pressure Tank Repair in North Lakes, AK

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in North Lakes

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation usually point to a faulty pressure tank.     Pressure Switch Replacement in North Lakes

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in North Lakes, AK

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Earthquakes are a real factor here, so flexible connectors and properly strapped water heaters help limit damage when the ground shakes. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.
