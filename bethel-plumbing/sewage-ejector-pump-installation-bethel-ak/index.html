    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Bethel

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are distinct. CKN Plumbing provides expert guidance and installation for both in Bethel. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, usually at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly mistakes.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Bethel

    Where wastewater contains tougher solids, a grinder pump installation in Bethel may be necessary. Unlike standard ejector pumps, grinder pumps macerate waste into a fine slurry before pumping it to the sewer line. This is often required for certain commercial applications or residential setups with specific waste disposal needs. CKN Plumbing can help you determine if a grinder pump is the right solution, handling the entire installation from start to finish, including the specialized electrical and discharge piping.
