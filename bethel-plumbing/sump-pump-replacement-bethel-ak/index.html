    It is a good idea to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit.     In many areas, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements usually do not require a permit.     While it may seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is highly recommended. It involves electrical connections, proper plumbing for the discharge line, and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding and comply with local Bethel codes.
