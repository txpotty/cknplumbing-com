    Ejector pumps typically last about 7 to 10 years, depending on use and what goes down the drains. Signs of trouble include constant running, frequent cycling, odors and slow fixture drainage.     Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Gateway

    The basin and pit are fundamental components of any sewage ejector pump system. CKN Plumbing provides expert installation of the sewage ejector pump basin and pit in Gateway, with proper sizing and sealing. Frost heave is a major concern in Alaska, as freezing ground can lift and shift foundations, buried lines, and septic components. A well-installed pit is key to longevity.
