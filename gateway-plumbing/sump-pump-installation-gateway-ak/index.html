    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Gateway

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often costs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups usually add $500 to $1,500.

    A well-maintained sump pump usually lasts between 7 and 10 years. The lifespan can vary depending on the quality of the pump, how frequently it runs and how well it is maintained. Regular inspections and cleaning can help extend its life.

    CKN Plumbing installs a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their durability and performance. Our common selections include Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty Pumps. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your particular needs and budget for your Gateway home.
