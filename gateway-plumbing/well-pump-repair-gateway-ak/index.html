    Jet Pump System Repair in Gateway, AK

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. CKN Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Gateway operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Gateway

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team carefully diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Gateway.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Gateway

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself.
