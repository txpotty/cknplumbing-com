    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Knik-Fairview

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary.     Tank Water Heater Flushing in Knik-Fairview

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.
