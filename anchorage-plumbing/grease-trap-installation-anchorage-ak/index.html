    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Anchorage, AK

    Correct sizing and strategic placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness.     Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Anchorage, AK

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Anchorage, AK. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to enhance separation efficiency. We provide professional installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for reliable performance and automated FOG removal.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Anchorage

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. CKN Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Anchorage for the best efficiency.
