    Backflow Preventer Testing in Anchorage

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Anchorage

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Anchorage

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Some rural households in Alaska use hauled water and storage tanks, which brings its own pump and freeze protection needs. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Preventative Leak Detection in Anchorage, AK

    Early leak detection is a key part of our plumbing maintenance. We use modern tools to find hidden leaks in pipes, especially those under slabs or behind walls, which are common in older homes. Many homes here have boilers, circulation pumps, and expansion tanks, which call for a plumber familiar with hydronic systems. Catching these leaks early prevents serious water damage, mold growth, and wasted water, saving homeowners in Anchorage from extensive repairs down the line.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Anchorage, AK

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a blockage. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your options.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Anchorage

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Anchorage, AK

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Anchorage, AK

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a written estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and peace of mind.
