    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Anchorage

    Sewer line cleaning in Anchorage usually costs $200 to $500 with a cable machine, or $400 to $900 with hydro jetting. Adding a recorded camera inspection usually runs $150 to $350.

    Hydro jetting is generally safe for most pipe materials, but it is crucial to have a professional assess your sewer line's condition first. We always perform a camera inspection to confirm your pipes can withstand the high-pressure water without damage, especially for very old or severely corroded lines.

    Rental machines can work on simple clogs, but they can kink or get stuck on roots. A pro also offers a camera to verify the line is clear.

    Lines with roots or past clogs often need cleaning every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines without issues may go years without service.

    Older homes often have clay or cast iron, and some have Orangeburg fiber pipe. Most homes built in recent decades have ABS or PVC. Much of the housing in Alaska was built during the 1970s and 1980s, so original copper and some polybutylene lines are now aging.

    While minor drain clogs can sometimes be handled with DIY methods, sewer line cleaning needs professional equipment like powerful augers or hydro jetters and expertise in diagnosing the problem. Trying to clear a main sewer line without proper tools can lead to further damage or ineffective results, so professional help is highly recommended.

    For most homes in Anchorage, annual or twice-yearly sewer line cleaning is sufficient, especially if you have mature trees nearby or an older plumbing system. If you have frequent clogs or a high-usage commercial property, more frequent cleaning might be wise to prevent major backups.

    We strongly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer line clogs. These products often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are usually ineffective against severe blockages like tree roots. They can also pose a health risk and make professional cleaning more difficult.
