    Pipe Bursting Services in Anchorage, AK

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is highly effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a solid solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Anchorage, AK.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Anchorage

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are often temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Anchorage, AK

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the most economical option. Pipe bursting or pulling a new line through the old path costs more but avoids open trenching and long-term risk.
