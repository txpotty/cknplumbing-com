    Proven Walls Waterproofing Solutions in Sterling, AK

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Sterling

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow significant water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points permanently. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can indicate structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and expensive future repairs.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Sterling, AK

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades prevent costly drywall repairs.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Sterling

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Frost heave is a major concern in Alaska, as freezing ground can lift and shift foundations, buried lines, and septic components. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Sterling

    Restaurants, gyms, salons and offices often have restrooms, locker rooms and kitchens with constant water exposure. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with durable membranes. Work can be scheduled after hours to limit downtime. We also repair the plumbing behind those walls when leaks are discovered.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Sterling

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Sterling, AK

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are frequent culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Sterling

    Much of the housing in Alaska was built during the 1970s and 1980s, so original copper and some polybutylene lines are now showing their age. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater builds up.

    Drainage System Integration in Sterling, AK

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Sterling, AK properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.
