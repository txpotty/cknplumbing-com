    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Sterling, AK

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method.     Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Sterling, AK

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel.     Slab Leak Reroute in Sterling

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Sterling
