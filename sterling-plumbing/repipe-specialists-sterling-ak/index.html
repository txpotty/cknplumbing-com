    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Sterling, AK

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are notorious for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply.     Efficient PEX Repiping in Sterling, AK

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Sterling, AK. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
