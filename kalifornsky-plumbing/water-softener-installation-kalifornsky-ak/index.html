    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Kalifornsky

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. CKN Plumbing expertly installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Kalifornsky home.
