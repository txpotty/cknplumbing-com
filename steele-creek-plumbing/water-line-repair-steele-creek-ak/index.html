    Main Water Line Repair in Steele Creek

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team focuses on diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to find the exact location of the problem, minimizing excavation and disruption to your property in Steele Creek.     Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Steele Creek

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Steele Creek, AK

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water.     Main Water Line Leak Repair in Steele Creek

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Steele Creek, AK

    Many older homes in Steele Creek, AK still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time.     Water Line Repair at the Meter in Steele Creek, AK
