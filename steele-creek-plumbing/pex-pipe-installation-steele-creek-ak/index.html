    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Steele Creek, AK

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Steele Creek

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Steele Creek

    A whole-home PEX repipe usually costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has fewer joints. Copper is very durable but can develop pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not form pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Steele Creek homes. It also cuts down on water hammer noise.

    PEX is well suited for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. A long, severe heating season is the defining plumbing stress in Alaska, and boilers and water heaters run nearly nonstop for much of the year. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, greatly lowering the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps. It also performs well in high temperatures, making it a reliable choice year-round in Steele Creek.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers commonly offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    CKN Plumbing stands behind our PEX pipe installations with a comprehensive warranty on both labor and materials. Specific warranty details will be provided with your estimate, but you can be confident your new PEX system is installed correctly and will perform reliably for years to come in your Steele Creek home.

    We mainly use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in Alaska plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is strongly recommended. Correct sizing, routing and secure connections are essential for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.
