    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Fairbanks

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Fairbanks, AK

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is essential to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and protecting system integrity.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Fairbanks, AK

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Fairbanks

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Fairbanks

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout.     Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Fairbanks
