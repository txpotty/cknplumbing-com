    CSST Flexible Gas Piping in Fairbanks, AK

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing installs faster than black iron and routes easily through attics and finished walls with fewer joints. CSST must be bonded to the home's grounding system to reduce lightning damage risk. We use brands with fittings our plumbers are certified to install and follow the manufacturer's instructions.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Installation in Fairbanks

    We use respected brands for gas line components, including Gastite and TracPipe for CSST, and high-quality black iron pipe from reputable manufacturers. We also install dependable shutoff valves and fittings from brands like Mueller and Nibco for durability and safety.

    We mainly install black iron pipe and corrugated stainless steel tubing, or CSST. Black iron is a traditional, rigid option, while CSST offers flexibility and faster installation. Both are safe and code-compliant when installed by licensed professionals, and the choice usually depends on the application and property layout.

    The most critical sign of a gas line issue is the smell of rotten eggs, which indicates a gas leak. Other signs include a hissing sound near a gas line, visible damage to a pipe or dead vegetation over an underground gas line. If you suspect a gas leak, evacuate immediately and call your gas provider and then us.

    Common materials include black iron pipe, which is very durable, and corrugated stainless steel tubing, which offers flexibility and can be faster to install. Copper tubing is sometimes used for smaller diameter lines but is less common for main lines. Our team chooses the best material for your application and local codes.

    CSST bonding connects the gas piping to the home's electrical grounding system to reduce damage from lightning strikes. It is a code and manufacturer requirement we follow on every install.
