    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Fairbanks, AK

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one.
