    Catch Basin Cleaning in Fairbanks, AK

    Catch basins are key parts of any storm drainage system in Fairbanks, AK, designed to collect runoff and trap debris before it enters the main drain lines. Regular cleaning of catch basins is important to prevent them from becoming overloaded and causing blockages further down the system. Our service includes removing built-up leaves, sediment, trash, and other debris from catch basins, so they work correctly and protect your entire drainage system. This preventative maintenance is key to heading off larger issues.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Fairbanks, AK

    Hydro jetting is a proven solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Fairbanks, AK. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is highly effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Fairbanks

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during a downpour.

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Fairbanks

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can carry a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and level.
