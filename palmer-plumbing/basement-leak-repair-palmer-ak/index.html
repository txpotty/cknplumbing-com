    Interior Drainage Systems in Palmer

    When exterior excavation isn't practical, interior drainage systems provide an effective solution for managing basement leaks. We install perimeter drains along the basement walls, collecting water before it can surface and directing it to a sump pump for removal. This system is less disruptive and can be a cost-effective way to dry out a wet basement.

    Emergency Basement Flooding Response in Palmer

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Palmer, AK

    For complete basement leak prevention, exterior waterproofing involves excavating around your foundation and applying waterproof membranes or coatings.     Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Palmer, AK

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale with age, and plastic joints can separate if a line was poorly supported. These leaks often show up only when an upstairs fixture runs. We run water through each drain while watching the basement, then confirm the crack with a camera. Repairs range from a single section swap to replacing an entire stack with PVC or ABS.

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Palmer, AK

    Water heaters, softeners and laundry machines are often installed in basements, and their leaks pool fast. A dripping T&P valve, a corroded tank bottom or a split washer hose are frequent culprits. We repair or replace the failed part and install drain pans and leak sensors to catch future problems early. If a tank has rusted through, we can replace it the same day in most cases.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Palmer

    A basement floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm usually points to a blockage or a failed trap. We clear the drain, check the line to the main sewer and replace cracked traps and covers. Installing a backwater valve stops sewage from pushing back into the basement if the main line surcharges. We set the valve where it can be serviced without trouble.
