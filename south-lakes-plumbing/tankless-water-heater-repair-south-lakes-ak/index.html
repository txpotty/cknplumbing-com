    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in South Lakes, AK

    Electric tankless units offer a compact solution but can run into problems with heating elements or circuit breakers.     Condensing Tankless Repair in South Lakes, AK

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

Our team in South Lakes, AK diagnoses issues with igniters, flame sensors and gas valves. We ensure proper gas pressure and ventilation are maintained for safe and efficient operation.
