    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in South Lakes, AK

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in South Lakes, AK

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in South Lakes
