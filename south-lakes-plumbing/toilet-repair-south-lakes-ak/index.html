    Fill Valve Replacement in South Lakes

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in South Lakes, AK

    Leaking Toilet Repair in South Lakes

    Toilet leaks can cause significant water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can start from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.
