    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Tanaina

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Tanaina

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Tanaina home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Bradford White & Rheem Tank Maintenance in Tanaina

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Tanaina, AK

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Tanaina, AK

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it affects the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Tanaina

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous.
